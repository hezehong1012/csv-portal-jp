Frequently asked questions

Why does it open a print dialog instead of downloading a PDF?
The PDF is produced by the browser's own print engine. That way system fonts are used and CJK characters never turn into boxes or missing glyphs, which is the usual failure mode of JavaScript PDF libraries. Choose “Save as PDF” as the print destination.
Will Japanese or Chinese text print correctly?
Yes. Rendering uses the fonts installed on your machine, so CJK text prints as you see it.
What about very wide CSVs?
The font size shrinks automatically. If it still does not fit, switch the paper to landscape or to A3.
How many rows can I print?
There is no hard limit, though previews above a few thousand rows can feel sluggish.
Is my data uploaded?
No. Everything from parsing to printing happens in your browser.
