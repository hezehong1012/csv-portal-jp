What makes it different

Mixed delimiters

A file that mixes commas and semicolons is normalised against the dominant delimiter.

Uneven column counts

Rows with too few columns get padded, rows with too many are trimmed, so the table lines up again.

Quote repair

Values that contain a delimiter or a line break but were never quoted get wrapped correctly.

Normalise formats

Full-width to half-width characters, trimming, dates unified to YYYY-MM-DD, and European decimals such as 1.234,56 converted.

Frequently asked questions

Does it overwrite my original file?
No. Your input stays untouched and the repaired data is offered as a separate download.
What kind of damage does it handle?
Mixed delimiters, inconsistent column counts, unbalanced quotes, full-width characters, stray whitespace, mixed date formats, European decimals, and mojibake detection.
Do I need the AI mode?
No. Every rule-based fix runs locally in your browser. The AI is only used when you want to describe a change in words.
Is it safe to paste confidential data?
Diagnosis and rule-based repair never leave your browser. Only if you use the AI instruction panel is the relevant data sent to the language model.
Will it restore garbled text automatically?
It detects and reports mojibake. Restoring it requires guessing the original encoding, which the dedicated garbled-text tool does.
